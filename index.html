Precision Water Delivery

Drip Irrigation System

Delivers water directly to plant roots through a network of valves, pipes, and emitters. Saves up to 60% more water compared to flood irrigation.

  • 90%+ Water Use Efficiency
  • Minimizes Weed Growth & Soil Erosion
  • Ideal for Solanaceous & Fruit Crops
Simulated Rainfall

Smart Sprinkler Systems

Pipes pressure water through nozzles to simulate natural rainfall. Equipped with automated moisture sensors for timed cycles.

  • Uniform Coverage for Dense Fields
  • Automated Rain Delay Controls
  • Excellent for Wheat, Pulses & Grains
IoT Moisture Control

Subsurface & Automated Pivot Systems

Integrates subterranean drip lines and automated central pivot booms with live soil sensors to deliver automated micro-irrigation schedules.

  • Zero Surface Evaporation Losses
  • Fully Remote Telemetry & App Triggering
  • Optimal for Large Commercial Farmlands

Soil Classification & Health

Understanding your soil profile is the foundation of successful agricultural operations.

pH 6.0 - 7.5

Alluvial Soil

Rich in potash, phosphoric acid, and lime. Highly fertile riverbed silt, ideal for rice, wheat, and sugarcane.

pH 6.8 - 8.0

Black (Regur) Soil

High clay content with exceptional moisture retention. Self-plowing texture, perfect for cotton, groundnut, and soybean.

pH 5.5 - 6.5

Red & Yellow Soil

Formed by crystalline igneous rocks, rich in iron oxide. Well-aerated, excellent for millets, pulses, and oilseeds.

pH 4.5 - 5.5

Laterite Soil

Leached acidic soil rich in iron and aluminum oxides. Highly suited for plantation crops like tea, coffee, and cashew.
